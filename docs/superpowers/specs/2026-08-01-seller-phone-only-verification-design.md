# Seller phone-only verification

## Goal

Simplify the seller publish requirements by removing email verification from the car-details form.

## Scope

- Remove the email verified requirement row.
- Remove the Send email link action.
- Keep phone verification and its SMS flow unchanged.

## Non-goals

- No changes to Firebase account settings or email verification elsewhere in the app.
- No changes to phone verification or publication validation beyond removing the email requirement UI.
